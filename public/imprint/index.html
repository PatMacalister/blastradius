Note on BlastRadius

BlastRadius is free software, provided without charge and without warranty. It is not a commercial service: there are no accounts, no paid tier, and no hosted component.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to GitHub, Stripe, Railway, Supabase, Vercel, Cloudflare, or any other provider whose credentials it inspects. It reads what those providers' own public APIs report about a credential and describes the result. Where it cannot determine a credential's privileges, it says so rather than estimating, and its output is a technical indicator — not a security audit, and not legal or compliance advice.
